
# Keepers
- Two players may be kept from a previous year's team for the following year. There is a 2-year contract on each keeper, meaning a player cannot be kept for more than 2 seasons.
- In order to keep a player, the team forfeits the draft pick one round earlier than they were selected in the prior year's draft.
- First round picks cannot be kept.
- If a keeper is picked up as a free agent (not drafted at all the year prior), the team will lose the last possible draft pick.
- If a player is drafted and then traded or released — the player, if kept, will still count as where they were originally drafted.
- If the round is not available to be lost due to trade, the pick prior is lost. If there are no rounds prior, then this player cannot be kept.
- If a player is traded, his contract does not reset.
- If a player is picked up from the waiver wire, then the contract DOES reset. This does NOT apply if a player is picked up by the same team that dropped him — the player must pass through waivers.

# Regular Season Matchups and Playoffs
- There are 14 regular season games.
- Playoffs start week 15 and run through week 17.
- 6 teams make the playoffs, across 3 rounds. Each round is one week.
- There is no tiebreaker for regular season games — games end in a tie.
- The tiebreaker for playoffs is head-to-head record.
- The top team in each of the 2 divisions makes the playoffs, plus 4 wildcard teams.

# Dues and Payouts
- Dues are $150 per team, paid out as:
- 1st place: $1,100
- 2nd place: $475
- 3rd place: $150
- An additional $50 survivor-pool payout — separate from the regular payouts and unaffected by wins/losses. Each week, the team with the fewest points among those remaining is eliminated; the last team standing wins $50.

# Draft / Lottery
- A modified lottery is used to determine the draft order. The 6 teams that did not make the playoffs in the prior year go into the first pot — the last-place team has the highest chance at the first pick, and so on.
- The winner of the consolation bracket (for teams that didn't make the playoffs) gets one additional ball in the lottery, regardless of their initial position.
- The 2 teams knocked out in the first round of the playoffs are placed in a lottery for picks 5-8, following the same rules as above.
- The final four teams pick in reverse order of how they finished, with the prior year's champion making the final pick of the first round and the first pick of the second round.

# Offseason Trades
- The trade window opens again when the commissioner activates the league (usually toward the middle of July).
- Both players and draft picks may be traded.
- No players may be picked up from the waiver wire during the offseason — players must have been on a team's roster at the end of the prior season to be kept.

# Penalties / Last Place
- The owner who finished in last place pays for the nameplate added to the trophy.

# Amendments
- Any changes to the constitution must be voted on and receive 8 out of 12 votes.
