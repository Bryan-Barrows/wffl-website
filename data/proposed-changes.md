# Rule Changes for Next Season

- Keepers: the owner will now lose the pick the player was drafted with last year, not the round prior. This has been voted on and will go into effect in 2027. (approved)
- Keepers: starting next year, keepers will be due 1 week before the draft instead of 1 day before the draft. Changes to keepers will be allowed for injuries, suspensions, and trades (approved)
- Moved to default sleeper playoff tiebreaker (points for) (approved)
- Lottery odds based on performance in losers bracket, not reverse standings (approved)
- Winner or lottery gets first choice of draft slot and so on (approved)
- Reduce the number of bench spots by 1 (proposed)
- allow 'out' players on IR for the week (proposed)
- move trade deadline to after week 9 (proposed)
