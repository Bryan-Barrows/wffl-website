# Rule Changes for Next Season

- Keepers: the owner will now lose the pick the player was drafted with last year, not the round prior. This has been voted on and will go into effect in 2027.
- Keepers: starting next year, keepers will be due 1 week before the draft instead of 1 day before the draft. Changes to keepers will be allowed for injuries, suspensions, and trades
- Moved to default sleeper playoff tiebreaker (points for)
- Lottery odds based on performance in losers bracket, not reverse standings
- Winner or lottery gets first choice of draft slot and so on
