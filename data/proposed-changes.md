# Rule Changes for Next Season

- Keepers: the owner will now lose the pick the player was drafted with last year, not the round prior. This has been voted on and will go into effect in 2027.
- Keepers: starting next year, keepers will be due 1 week before the draft instead of 1 day before the draft. Changes to keepers will be allowed for injuries and trades
- Choose new conferences
